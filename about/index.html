Kacy Ltd runs three businesses: Kacy Farms under the brand KOKOLIKOOO, Kacy Motel for hospitality, and commercial real estate.

Our Vision

To become a leading supplier of premium poultry products in Rwanda and regional, recognized for quality, sustainability, customer satisfaction and empowering women and youth in agribusiness.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, fresh, and sustainably raised poultry meat through ethical, innovative, and inclusive farming practices that contribute to food security, customer satisfaction, and local economic development.
