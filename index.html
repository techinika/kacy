FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kacy Farms' annual poultry production capacity?

We produce 150 tons of fresh chicken every year, and we're growing. Our plan is to go from 6,000 to 20,000 birds to keep up with demand.

Do you deliver fresh chicken to Kigali hotels and supermarkets?

Yes. We deliver fresh, chilled chicken across Kigali within 24 hours. Hotels, supermarkets, restaurants; if you run a kitchen, we can help.

What products does Kacy Farms offer?

Whole chickens, drumsticks, wings, thighs, fillets; and if you need something custom, just ask. We also offer wholesale contracts for regular buyers.

Where is Kacy Farms located?

We're based in Gasabo District, Rusororo, Kigali. Our farm and processing facility are both on-site, so everything happens in one place.
