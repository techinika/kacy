How to Choose a Reliable Chicken Supplier in Kigali

Whether you run a hotel restaurant, a supermarket, or a catering business, the chicken you serve reflects your standards. Here are five checks that separate a supplier you can depend on from one that leaves you scrambling for last-minute alternatives.

1. Ask About Volume and Lead Times

A reliable supplier knows how much chicken they can commit to, week after week. Ask directly: how many birds or how many kilograms can you guarantee per order? What happens when demand spikes? At KOKOLIKOOO, we produce 150 tons of fresh chicken every year across 12 yearly batches and deliver across Kigali within 24 hours, so you never have to wonder whether your next delivery will clear your daily specials.

2. Inspect the Cold Chain

"Fresh" only matters if the chicken stays cold from farm to door. Visit the facility if you can; otherwise ask about transport temperature, refrigeration units, and how long the product spends in transit. Chicken that warms even once degrades in texture and safety. We transport every order in refrigerated trucks and keep the entire chain — from processing to your receiving dock — temperature-controlled.

What Temperature Is "Cold Enough"?

Whole birds should arrive at or below 4°C (40°F). Any higher and you're flirting with bacterial growth. A supplier that measures and verifies this, every run, is one worth keeping.

3. Check Food Safety Hygiene

Hygiene isn't just a sticker on the wall. Ask for certifications like HACCP and RSB (Rwanda Standards Board) status, and confirm the team follows documented procedures for cleaning, handling, and traceability. At KOKOLIKOOO we're working toward full certification because safety is non-negotiable in any professional kitchen.

4. Confirm Consistent Cuts and Weights

Nothing throws a recipe off like inconsistent piece sizes. A good supplier cuts to order and keeps weights predictable so your team can portion accurately and your inventory planning stays reliable. We portion chicken the way your kitchen needs it — by the cut and by the case.

5. Test Responsiveness

Before you sign a contract, send an inquiry. A reliable supplier answers quickly, understands bulk needs, and makes ordering regular deliveries straightforward. You shouldn't need a project manager to buy chicken. If you can reach us within minutes by phone or WhatsApp, you'll get that same responsiveness when you need an extra delivery.
